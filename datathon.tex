The \textbf{Channel Islands} are a chain of eight islands located off
the southern coast of California in the Pacific Ocean. They are known
for their stunning natural beauty, ecological richness, and deep

\section{\texorpdfstring{Island Fox (\emph{Urocyon
littoralis})}{Island Fox (Urocyon littoralis)}}\label{island-fox-urocyon-littoralis}

The \textbf{island fox} is a small, charismatic canid species
\emph{endemic} to six of the eight \emph{Channel Islands} off the coast
of southern California. It's one of the best examples of \textbf{island
dwarfism}, having evolved from the mainland gray fox (\emph{Urocyon
cinereoargenteus}) to become significantly smaller.

In the \textbf{1990s}, several island fox populations faced \textbf{near
extinction} due to golden eagle predation, canine distemper virus, and
habitat degradation. The island fox was listed as endangered in 2004.
Recovery efforts of the island fox included removal of golden eagles,
vaccination and captive breeding programs, reintroduction of native bald
eagles, and habitat restoration.

\section{Data and Prep}\label{intro}

The data provided is part of an ongoing longitudinal study of island
foxes from 2014 to 2016 of trap monitoring. This resulted in collection
of 4,975 recorded trap observations, which may have contained a fox or
not. Other information collected were Sex, Age Class, Weight, Body
Condition, Reproductive Status, and Vaccinations. Information on the
data collected can be found in the
\href{https://github.com/mludkov/UCSB_Data_Day_2026/blob/gh-pages/data_for_datathon_2014_2016.csv}{Databook}.

\subsubsection{Time Series}\label{time-series}

While above we considered the general \textbf{population} trends of how
foxes are across the years, this evidence is not so conclusive because
potentially it is about different foxes. A stronger analysis would
directly track the same fox across time. \emph{Time series}, where we
put the date (in our case \texttt{TrapDate}) on the x-axis and the
numeric variable of interest (say \texttt{Weight}) on the y-axis serve
this purpose. To be interesting however, the time span needs to be long
enough -- it is not so interesting to make a time series for fox
989001000124022 that was only ever observed over a single week in 2016.
